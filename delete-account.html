Delete Your ScapeAI Care Account

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the app:

  1. Open ScapeAI Care
  2. Go to Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm deletion in the dialog

This will permanently delete your account, all plant scan history, and associated data. This action cannot be undone.

If you are unable to access the app, email us at info@scapeai.in with subject Account Deletion Request and we will process it within 7 business days.
