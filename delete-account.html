Delete your ScapeAI Care account

ScapeAI Care is an app by Scape AI (Gautami Ankit Ghotikar, Vadodara, India). You can delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open ScapeAI Care and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm in the dialog

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't use the app, email info@scapeai.in with the subject Account Deletion Request, from or mentioning the mobile number you signed in with. We will verify it's you and delete your account within 7 business days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, mobile number sign-in and profile (name)
  • Plant scans, scan photos and results
  • Saved plants and care reminders
  • Saved addresses
  • Remaining credits (these cannot be refunded or transferred)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Gardener visit bookings and payment records (date, service, address, amount) are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require, normally up to 8 years, and to resolve any disputes. They are then deleted.
  • Google Play purchase records are kept by Google under its own policy.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are kept for Google Firebase's standard periods (up to 90 days for crash reports and 14 months for analytics) and are not linked to your name or number.

Delete some data without deleting your account

In the app you can delete individual saved addresses (Profile → Saved Addresses), reminders and plants at any time. For anything else, email info@scapeai.in.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
