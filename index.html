Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 May 2025

At Scape AI, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ScapeAI Care app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name and email address when you create an account. We also collect images you upload for plant diagnosis, care reminders you set, and device information necessary to deliver the service (device type, operating system version, and app crash reports).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our AI plant diagnosis service
  • Send you care reminders and in-app notifications you have opted into
  • Respond to your comments and support requests
  • Monitor and analyse usage patterns to improve app performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Plant Images & AI Processing

Images you submit for diagnosis are sent to our AI processing servers to identify your plant and assess its health. Processed images may be retained for a limited period to improve our AI model. We do not sell or share your plant images with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers who assist us in operating the app (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics)
  • Legal authorities where required by law or to protect our rights

All third-party service providers are contractually bound to protect your data.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and scan history for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at info@scapeai.in. Anonymised and aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for research and model improvement.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (TLS) and secure cloud storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@scapeai.in.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or via email. Your continued use of the app after the effective date of the updated policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes.
