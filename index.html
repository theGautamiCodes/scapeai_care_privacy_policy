Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ScapeAI Care is owned and operated by Gautami Ankit Ghotikar, a sole proprietor trading as Scape AI, based in Vadodara, Gujarat, India. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the ScapeAI Care app collects, how we use it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It covers both our AI plant care features and our gardener (maali) visit service.

1. Information We Collect

Account

  • Your mobile number, used to sign in with a one-time code
  • Your name and any profile details you choose to add

Plant care

  • Photos you take or upload to identify or diagnose plants, and the results
  • Plants you save, care reminders you set and your scan history

Gardener visit bookings

  • Your service address: house or flat number, street, area, landmark, city, pincode and an optional label such as Home or Office
  • The map pin you place, which gives the exact location of your door
  • Visit details: number of plants, type of space, date and time slot, problem description and any photos you attach
  • Booking history, status, price and payment method
  • Saved addresses, if you choose to save them
  • Care plan requests, and waitlist requests for areas we do not serve yet

Location

  • If you allow location access, your device location is used to show local weather, to place the map pin near you when you add an address and, optionally, to improve plant identification for your region. Location is only used while you are using these features, never in the background, and we do not keep a history of your location.

Device and usage

  • Device type, operating system version, app version, crash reports and anonymous usage statistics
  • Device integrity checks (Google Play Integrity) that confirm requests come from the genuine app
  • A notification token so we can send reminders and booking updates

Purchases

  • Records of credit packs or subscriptions bought through Google Play. We only receive a purchase token; we never see your card, UPI or bank details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Identify and diagnose plants and show care guidance
  • Book, schedule and carry out gardener visits, and contact you about them
  • Share your address and visit details with the gardener assigned to your visit
  • Check whether we serve your area, and tell you when we start serving it if you joined the waitlist
  • Manage your credits and verify purchases
  • Send reminders, booking confirmations and service updates
  • Provide support, including over WhatsApp when you contact us there
  • Prevent fraud and misuse and keep the app secure
  • Fix problems and improve the app
  • Meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your address or photos for advertising.

3. Plant Images & AI Processing

  • When you scan a plant, the photo is sent securely to Plant.id (Kindwise s.r.o.) for identification and health assessment. Plant.id deletes the photo from its servers after processing.
  • When you search the plant library, only the category text you search for is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to suggest plant names. No photos, location or personal details are sent.
  • Your scan photos are stored in Google Firebase Storage, encrypted at rest. Only you can see them.

We do not use your plant photos to train AI models, and we do not sell or share them for marketing.

4. Gardener Visits & Your Address

  • Your address, map pin, visit details and booking photos are seen only by our operations team and the gardener assigned to your visit, and only to plan and carry out that visit.
  • The gardener or our team may call or message you on your registered mobile number about the visit.
  • Our team may take before-and-after photos of the work as a record of the visit.
  • Saved addresses stay in your account so you can book faster. You can edit or delete them at any time from Profile → Saved Addresses.

5. Data Sharing

We share information only with the people and services needed to run the app:

  • Our team and gardeners — booking details, as described above
  • Plant.id by Kindwise — plant photos and, optionally, approximate location, for identification and diagnosis
  • Anthropic — plant search text only
  • Google Firebase — sign-in and phone number verification, database, file storage, server functions, notifications, crash reports, usage statistics and app integrity checks
  • Google Maps Platform — showing the map, searching for places and turning your map pin into an address
  • Google Play — in-app purchases and Play Integrity checks
  • Open-Meteo — approximate location coordinates only, to fetch local weather
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — only if you choose to contact us on WhatsApp

We may also disclose information if required by law or a valid request from authorities, or to protect the rights and safety of our users, gardeners and Scape AI. If Scape AI is merged or acquired, your information may be transferred under this policy.

6. Data Retention

  • Photos sent to Plant.id are deleted from their servers after processing.
  • Your account, scan history, plants, reminders and saved addresses are kept while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account). This deletes your profile, scans and photos, plants, reminders and saved addresses.
  • Booking and payment records may be kept after your account is deleted for as long as Indian tax, accounting and other laws require, or to resolve disputes.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are kept for Google’s standard retention periods.
  • Anonymised, aggregated data that cannot identify you may be kept to improve the service.

7. Security

We protect your information with:

  • Encrypted connections (TLS) for all data sent to and from the app
  • Google Firebase storage, encrypted at rest
  • Access rules so only you can see your scans and photos, and only you, our team and your assigned gardener can see your booking details
  • App integrity checks to block requests that do not come from the genuine app
  • API keys kept on our servers, never inside the app

No system is completely secure. Never share your sign-in code with anyone — our team will never ask for it.

  • Anthropic — anthropic.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform, Google Play — policies.google.com/privacy

9. Children’s Privacy

ScapeAI Care is meant for adults. Gardener visits must be booked by someone aged 18 or over. Anyone under 18 may use the app only with the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child without that consent, we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update your data — most details can be edited directly in the app
  • Ask us to delete your data, or delete your account in the app
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (this may stop some features, such as bookings, from working)
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied

To use any of these rights, email info@scapeai.in from your registered details or contact us in the app.
